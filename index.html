Questions and answers

The things people ask us most.

How fast will I get my song?

Within 2 hours of confirming your order and payment.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Each package includes free revisions, from 1 to unlimited. Tell us what to change and we send a new version.

Can the song include names and personal details?

Yes. Send names, dates and short stories in your idea and the musician will weave them into the lyrics.

Who owns the song?

You can use any track for personal projects and social media. The Exclusive package gives you full exclusive rights.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We reply to your order with the payment options available in US dollars, such as bank transfer or a card link.

What if I do not like the result?

Write to us within 24 hours. We will fix it or agree a fair solution with you.
